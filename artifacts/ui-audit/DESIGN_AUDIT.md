# Super Rogues — Independent Visual & UX Design Audit (Phase 2)

**Baseline Commit:** `7794de7`  
**Audit Basis:** Direct visual inspection of Playwright screenshots in `artifacts/ui-audit/before/` across 5 viewports (`desktop-1440x900`, `desktop-1920x1080`, `tablet-768x1024`, `mobile-390x844`, `mobile-375x812`) plus `before/report.json` and `artifacts/ui-audit/SCREEN_INVENTORY.md`.

---

## Executive Critique Across Product Dimensions

### A. Visual Hierarchy
- **Competing Focal Points & Broken Primary CTAs:** Primary calls to action fail to stand out consistently. On desktop hero pick (`09-start-hero-selected`), `SELECT ROHAMMER ➔` renders dark teal while on mobile deploy (`20-deploy-ready`) the main `Fight!` button turns flat muted slate-gray. Conversely, secondary actions and non-interactive plaques (shop `Sell Bag`, every shop item/hero price button, all three Boss Awakening path buttons, Crown Shop `0 crowns` header banner, and yellow `Medium fight` banner stripes) seize the reserved action-yellow hue.
- **Post-Battle Hierarchy Collapse:** Desktop `22-result` renders three disconnected boxes where the right-hand `.rcol-damage` panel repeats `DAMAGE DEALT` that is already shown in the left `STATS` bars, while the primary CTA `< PROCEED >` sits in a dark teal frame instead of commanding clear visual closure. On mobile/tablet `22-result`, concatenated headers (`STATSHERO PERFORMANCE`, `RESULTSREWARDS`) and an unstyled right void break scanning order.

### B. Typography
- **Font Family & Pairing Drift:** Although `Fredoka` and `Inter` are bundled locally, `style-screens.css` and `style.css` fall back to `'Cinzel', 'Trajan Pro', Georgia, serif` (unbundled webfont fallback rendering system `Georgia` serif on `22-result` `BATTLE WON` and `.sc` headers), creating a jarring clash between playful arcade rounded display (`Fredoka` on the `Play` button and title logo) and stiff Georgia serif headings.
- **Label Collisions & Micro-Wrapping:** Hero card role tags in `14-hero-shop` squeeze `From afar`/`Up close` into awkward 2-line pills beneath portraits, `.rpanel-head` concatenates two uppercase labels with zero gap, top-bar `lvchip` mislabels account level as `"LP"` on the landing page, and `card.html` shows `"Tester"` colliding beneath the champion badge on mobile.

### C. Layout and Spacing
- **Critical Fold Clipping (`11-map` Desktop):** `#wmap` renders scrolled to `scrollTop: 0` (Day 10–16 castle peak) while Day 1's clickable route banners sit at `y = 1556–1966px` completely outside the `588px` scroll viewport—players cannot see or click their available paths without manually hunting downward.
- **Viewport Overflows & Zero-Scroll Violations:** `mobile-390x844` and `mobile-375x812` overflow vertically on `18-boss-upgrade` (`858 > 844`, `877 > 812`, clipping the 3rd upgrade path), all 3 shop types (`+6px`), deploy (`+6px`), and battle (`+6px`). Desktop viewports (`1440x900` and `1920x1080`) overflow vertically on `start-hero` (`+40px`), `map` (`+208px`/`+384px`), `deploy` (`+98px`), and `battle` (`+56px`), masked only by `overflow: hidden`.
- **Canvas Obstruction on Desktop (`19-deploy`, `21-battle`):** Floating HUD panels (`.dleft-dock`, `.foes`, `#bpanel`, `.dparty`, `.bench`) are positioned directly over canvas space, overlapping enemy sprites on top-right and hero hexes at the bottom even at `1920×1080`.
- **Broken Width Containers (`22-result`, `16b-hero-info`, `27-card-page`):** Mobile/tablet `22-result` occupies only ~65% left width with a black void on the right; `hero-info` renders inside a 2-column `.gearsheet` leaving half the modal blank and stacking two `✕` close buttons; `card.html` staggers two hero cards vertically instead of a balanced row.

### D. Color and Visual Identity
- **Yellow Token Dilution (`AGENTS.md` Rule Violation):** Saturated yellow appears across alerts, buy buttons, `Sell Bag`, wallet banners, map banners, and multi-option path buttons, erasing the instant visual contract that yellow = primary forward action / key reward.
- **Identity Fragmentation:** At least four distinct visual languages coexist across one product: arcade fantasy (`01-title`), dark obsidian tabletop (`11-map`), neo-brutalist indigo/neon HUD (`14-hero-shop`, `18-boss-upgrade`), serif dungeon scroll (`22-result`), and unstyled light-mode Bootstrap-esque white tables (`26-stats-page`).

### E. Components
- **Broken `[data-act=team]` Flow:** Clicking Team/Equip on map, shop, or deploy triggers either a dismissive toast or a no-op instead of opening Gear Management, stranding bag inspection and dragging UX.
- **Broken Form Control (`23-gauntlet-gate`):** `#tname` stretches to ~96px height next to inline action buttons on desktop, looking like a broken dark box rather than a text input.
- **Unstyled Third Column (`22-result` Desktop):** `.rcol-damage`, `.rcd-head`, `.rcd-tabs`, `.rcd-row` ship in JS markup with zero corresponding CSS rules.
- **Conflicting Progress Bars (`24-over-lp`, `25-over-xp`):** LP screen shows BOTH an awkward oval mini-thermometer inside the shield card AND a duplicate horizontal meter whose center label (`45/100 LP`) straddles the high-contrast fill edge; XP screen does the same (`55/100 XP`) and clips `"New hero"` at the bottom of `.loot-card-3d`.

### F. UX and Usability
- **Coach/Tutorial Collision:** On `14-hero-shop`, `.coach.shopcoach` sits directly on top of the sticky bottom footer (`New` and `Continue` buttons). On `20-deploy-ready` mobile, `.coach` and `.tutmove` stack right over the frontline board hexes. On `08-start-hero-empty` desktop, `👆` sits on top of `CONTROLLER • RANGED`.
- **Dead-End First-Time Rewards Tab (`07b-rewards`):** Clicking `Rewards` (`[data-act=player-tab]`) as a new player opens an empty lockout message `"Rankings start with your first clear"` instead of showing the account level & unlock roadmap players clicked to inspect.
- **Redundant Controls (`07c-profile`):** Two separate `Crown Shop` buttons appear in the same modal alongside two awkwardly centered link buttons.
- **Console Errors:** Every load logs `GET /js/uiAudio.js` 404 MIME error (`21` errors across 5 viewports) due to an orphaned `<script>` tag in `index.html:33`.

### G. Responsive Design
- **Unadapted Breakpoints:** Mobile battle strip (`#tstrip`) allows portrait badges/items to encroach on ability bar text; tablet `18-boss-upgrade` pins content left leaving a dead empty right third; mobile `01-title` `.app-ver` (`v1.12`) overlaps the `Ranks` dock button; `stats.html` has no mobile table wrapping or return navigation.

### H. Product Quality
Currently, Super Rogues shows genuinely charming artwork, rich hero/item mechanics, and expressive combat—but fragmented CSS overrides (`!important` warfare across three stylesheets), unstyled/orphaned desktop panels, fold-clipping bugs, and yellow overuse undermine commercial polish and player trust. Consolidating onto one coherent dark-slate obsidian/arcade-fantasy design system with crisp hierarchy and bug-free responsive geometry will elevate it immediately to commercial indie/mobile-strategy grade.
